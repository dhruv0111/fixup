# Phase 7 metric definitions and date basis

All monetary values are computed server-side in integer paise, displayed in INR. A screenshot/submission is never a collection. No hardcoded business/demo values feed these metrics. Empty aggregates are calculated zeros; missing costs are null/incomplete, never invented zeros. Date presets use BusinessSettings.timezone, default Asia/Kolkata; database sessions use UTC so timestamps represent correct instants. CSV timestamp values are unambiguous ISO UTC (`Z`); date-only expense values are business calendar dates.

Flow metrics intersect the selected business-date range, ending at the earlier of the next local midnight after To date and report knowledge cutoff. Stock is an as-of snapshot at that cutoff. Daily/weekly/monthly groups use the same business timezone; weeks begin Monday. Financial-year preset means April 1–March 31. Today/month dashboard cards also intersect the selected range, as their labels state; choosing yesterday therefore makes the today-intersection card zero.

## Approved cost policy and history

The owner approved recorded-lot costing in conversation before implementation: original purchase line subtotal minus recorded discount, excluding separately recorded tax, divided by ordered quantity. The integer numerator/denominator is preserved, and each allocated dispatched quantity gets a frozen half-up paise amount. A fully discounted supported purchase may legitimately cost zero; an unknown opening/correction lot never becomes zero-cost by assumption. Rounding per allocation/remaining lot can differ by a paise from rounding a combined rational total; unit cost is not prematurely rounded.

Existing receipt-linked lots and posted dispatch allocations are backfilled only from immutable original purchase records, explicitly marked legacy. No business prices, costs, stock quantities, bank transactions or expense records are seeded. New lots/postings capture snapshots transactionally. Purchase/stock/dispatch audit records already cover their source actions; expense changes add immutable revisions plus financial audit metadata. Both the expense header and revision must correspond; ghost revisions and changes without history are rejected by database guards.

Financial amounts and expense views can be reproduced using the returned knowledgeAsOf. Current administrative flags/status distributions remain explicitly current, not a fictitious complete historical status warehouse. Physical returns/courier failure do not imply an automatic financial reversal. Returned shipments can make the estimate incomplete; a full returns/accounting module is outside scope.

## Bounds and historical timezone caveat

Reports refuse more than 10,000 matching source rows instead of presenting partial totals; narrow date/entity filters. Customer/supplier/variant selectors have documented bounds. Ready eligibility checks are bounded to 1,000 candidates. Tables paginate 25 rows; charts show up to 36 periods and labelled top entities, with complete matched records in CSV. Up to 10 years per query, calendar dates from 2000 onward, and no future To date. Scanned expense attachments are private images, up to five per expense and 5 MiB/16 megapixels each.

The installed Prisma adapter emits offset-free timestamps and normalizes returned offsets. UTC database sessions are now explicit, with business timezone conversion performed by reporting code. Existing stored records are not shifted. Pre-existing local records written under the former non-UTC session need interpretation review before any real historical production use; the verified development business tables are empty, and legacy retained test records are synthetic. This caveat is not hidden by changing historical values.
