# UX before/after screenshot evidence

All captures are private local QA evidence under `.local/ux-audit/`, not production/marketing assets. They contain synthetic accounts, references, amounts and stock. Do not deploy the directory or interpret its figures as real business results. Run paths are timestamped; baseline files were not replaced.

Document heights above 8,000 CSS pixels are explicitly marked `capture.mode="viewport"` in JSONL rather than pretending the entire page was captured. Legacy/new journey screenshots can use device-pixel scale; compare their logical viewport and workflow, not raw bitmap dimensions. Dummy fixture IDs/order numbers differ across runs. No pixel-identical business-content comparison is claimed.

## Failure and nonvisual evidence

The original full run's failure attachments are archived under `.local/test-evidence/1794661973188-b7ad1c6e-2fda-4051-956d-e1c0d85a4922/after/test-results/`. These include omitted price/supplier selections and the inventory capture protocol error; no missing screenshot was invented. Source/dimension diagnoses are in `.local/ux-audit/price-selector-diagnosis.json` and `supplier-selector-diagnosis.json`.

`full-regression-results.json` retains 309 passes/seven failures. `affected-final-results.json` (28/28), `inventory-final-results.json` (37/37), `stable-journeys-results.json` (13/13), and `latest-coverage.json` establish all 316 latest passes. Atlas geometry/axe output retains 48 incomplete contrast checks and inline target heuristics; a zero violation array is not blanket accessibility certification.

Loading-layout evidence is numerical: `performance-1794667065229.json` names FOOTER shift sources/rectangles; final `performance-1794668232547.json` shows no footer shifts/overflow across twelve production-build samples. No fake loading screenshot or field-performance claim substitutes for those actual entries.

This audit preserves its own baseline and successive archives; original reports, saved JSON/logs, malformed fixtures and backup evidence remain.
